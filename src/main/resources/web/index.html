Formal Module Distribution & 2-Minute Viva Voce Cheat-Sheet

S1

Student 1: Profile & Drive Registry Lead

Module 1: Academic Data & CSV Persistence
  • Candidate registration & academic records management (CGPA, backlogs, skills)
  • Company drive specifications (CTC, minimum cutoffs, eligible branches)
  • CSV-based file persistence in data/ folder
Source Files: Student.java, CompanyDrive.java, DataStore.java, StudentPanel.java
Viva Defense Answer: "We used local CSV files in the data directory so the portal is lightweight, 100% offline, and easily inspected by the college placement officer in Excel without database installation."
S2

Student 2: Eligibility Engine Lead

Module 2: Automated Criteria Evaluation
  • Automated multi-factor evaluation (CGPA threshold, active backlogs cap)
  • Department whitelist checking (allowing CSE, ISE, ECE)
  • College Dream Company Policy enforcement (1.5x salary package upgrade rule)
Source Files: EligibilityService.java, EligibilityPanel.java
Viva Defense Answer: "I built the eligibility service. It verifies that unplaced students meet the cutoffs, and prevents already-placed students from applying unless the new drive offers at least 1.5x their existing package."
S3

Student 3: Pipeline & Workflow Lead

Module 3: Interview Stages & Automated Offers
  • Candidate application lifecycle tracking across 7 recruitment rounds
  • Interview date and slot scheduling
  • Automated placement status synchronization upon offer issuance
Source Files: Application.java, PipelinePanel.java
Viva Defense Answer: "I built the candidate pipeline. When an applicant's stage is moved to OFFERED, the system automatically marks the candidate as PLACED with their package and updates institutional statistics."
S4

Student 4: Analytics & Full-Stack Lead

Module 4: Institutional KPIs & Desktop/Web GUI
  • Placement rate %, highest package, average CTC, and department statistics
  • MainFrame Swing GUI integration with tabbed panes
  • Embedded Java HTTP server and one-click CSV export
Source Files: AnalyticsService.java, MainFrame.java, WebServer.java, DashboardPanel.java
Viva Defense Answer: "I developed the analytics engine and GUI. It aggregates live placement percentages and allows T&P coordinators to export placed lists directly to CSV format."
